# Using QuoteLens

## Upload

Sign in and choose **Start a Comparison**. Give the purchase a name you will recognize later, then continue to upload. Choose 2–5 PDF, PNG, JPG or JPEG quotations, up to 10 MB each. Files are private to your account.

After uploading, choose **Read Quotation** next to each file. When all files are ready, choose **Review Quotation Details**. If reading fails, use **Try Again**.

## Review

Check each vendor's details against the quotation. The form groups all editable fields into vendor details, products and prices, taxes and other charges, delivery and warranty, and things to check. Use the vendor links to move between quotations.

Correct any errors and choose **Confirm Quotation Details**. Required calculation details are listed near the confirmation button. After every quotation is confirmed, the comparison opens automatically.

## Compare

Review the lowest total cost, fastest delivery, longest warranty and things to check. These labels describe confirmed details; they are not vendor recommendations. Costs are compared only when enough information is available and currencies match.

The tables show total costs, the totals written in quotations, commercial terms and individual items. Choose **Review Quotation Details** to make corrections.

## Export

Choose **Print / Export PDF** and select your printer or Save as PDF. The print layout hides navigation and controls and fits the report to A4 landscape pages. Disable browser headers and footers if you do not want the URL and date printed.

## Demo and verification

The public `/demo` uses fictional quotations and needs no account. The README screenshot reflects this demo. Automated browser tests cover its four-step guide, small-screen layout, print visibility and sign-in redirects. The signed-in upload and review screens should also be checked with a configured account before deployment.
